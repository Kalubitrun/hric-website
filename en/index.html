About the school

A neighbourhood institution for rural students

Haridwar Rai Inter College was established in 2000 and is recognised by the Uttar Pradesh Board of High School and Intermediate Education (UP Board). It is a privately managed, co-educational institution offering Hindi-medium education from class 6 to class 12.

The college exists so that students from the surrounding villages can complete their schooling close to home instead of travelling to the city.

Facilities include physics and chemistry laboratories, a library and book bank, clean drinking water and handwashing facilities, a generator for power backup, and digital boards in classrooms.
